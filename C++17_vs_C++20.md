# C++17 vs C++20 Compatibility

Rough estimate of how much of `src/includes/` relies on C++20-only features.

## Summary

**~39 of 108 files (~36%)** in `src/includes/` use at least one C++20-only
feature. Porting the library to C++17 would not be a small patch — it would
be a substantial refactor touching a third of the codebase directly, plus
anything that transitively includes those headers.

## Features used and their distribution

| Feature | Files | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `requires` clauses / concepts | ~15 | Constrained templates, e.g. `Numbers/PrimeDispatcher.h`, `Delays/ParallelPlainDelay.h` |
| `std::span` | ~12 | `AudioFile/SaveWav.h`, `AudioFile/LoadOgg.h`, `Audio/AudioBuffer.h`, several Delay/Reverb/Sampler classes |
| `std::ranges` | ~15 | Algorithm calls across Analysis, Generators, Reverbs, Sampler |
| `operator<=>` (three-way comparison) | 1 | `Audio/AudioBuffer.h` (`FrameIterator`) |

Not found in the codebase: `std::format`, `consteval`, `std::to_array`,
`[[likely]]`/`[[unlikely]]`, `std::jthread`, `constinit`, `using enum`.

## Hotspots

C++20 usage is concentrated in:

- `Analysis/`
- `Delays/`
- `Reverbs/` (especially the SIMD/Walsh FDN variants)
- `Sampler/`
- `SamplerateConverter/`
- `AudioFile/`

## What a C++17 downgrade would require

- Replace `std::span` with pointer+size pairs or a custom span polyfill.
- Replace `requires` clauses with SFINAE / `static_assert`-based constraints.
- Replace `std::ranges::*` algorithm calls with `std::*` + explicit iterator
  pairs.
- Replace `operator<=>` with explicit `==`, `<`, etc.

Given how interconnected the headers are (filters, wavetables, diffusers,
etc. all pull in these headers transitively), the effective blast radius is
larger than the 39 files identified directly — a downgrade would likely
touch most of the library.

## Conclusion

C++20 is load-bearing for this codebase. Downgrading to C++17 is feasible
but represents a significant refactor, not a quick configuration change.
